Phase 2: Problem Statement & HMW Framework

Core Problem Statement

"Indian railway travelers experience high stress, visual fatigue, and long checkout delays on IRCTC, leading to failed bookings during high-stakes Tatkal travel windows."

HMW #1: Tatkal Turbo Speed

How might we eliminate passenger typing friction so users can check out in under 60 seconds?

HMW #2: Predictive Status Transparency

How might we predict waiting list confirmation percentages to reduce travel uncertainty?

User Flow Optimization (11 Steps ➔ 4 Steps)

Legacy IRCTC Flow (11 Steps - 4.5 Mins)

  • 1. Homepage Ad Interstitial
  • 2. Manual Login & CAPTCHA 1
  • 3. Station Search Form
  • 4. Train Selection Grid
  • 5. Manual Passenger Entry (Name, Age, Gender, ID)
  • 6. CAPTCHA 2 Verification
  • 7. Multi-tab Payment Options

Redesigned Flow (4 Steps - 85 Secs)

  • 1. Smart Station Search + Tatkal Turbo Mode
  • 2. Train Selection with AI Confirmation %
  • 3. Visual Berth Picker + Saved Passenger Autofill
  • 4. 1-Tap Checkout & Digital QR E-Ticket
